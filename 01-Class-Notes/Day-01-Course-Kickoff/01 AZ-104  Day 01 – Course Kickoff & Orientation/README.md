## Important Study Clarifications

- **CapEx and OpEx:** later hardware purchases are not automatically OpEx. Classification depends on the nature of the expenditure and applicable accounting policy.
- **SLA examples:** the timings in the short notes are classroom examples, not universal commitments. The P3 resolution wording should be clarified.
- **Public/private IP addresses:** “local = free” and “public = paid” are shorthand, not complete technical definitions or universal charging rules.
- **Certification and support levels:** a certification does not automatically establish an employer's L1, L2, or L3 role.

Use the corrected study notes and relevant official documentation when resolving technical questions. Preserve the original class materials as source documents.
